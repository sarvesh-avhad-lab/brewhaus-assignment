OUR STORY

Small-batch roasting, done properly

Brewhaus started with a 5 kg roaster and a simple idea: coffee tastes best in the first few weeks after roasting. So we built a subscription around freshness, sourcing every lot ourselves and roasting it only when you need it.

Direct trade with 18 farms across three continents

Roasted in small batches every Monday and Thursday

Compostable packaging with one-way freshness valves

A printed brew guide in every box
